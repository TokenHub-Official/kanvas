# Code of Conduct

The Kanvas community expects professional, respectful, and evidence-based
collaboration.

- Discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks, harassment,
  discrimination, or degrading language.
- Respect differences in experience, language, background, and accessibility
  needs.
- Make code-review feedback actionable by explaining impact, risk, and a
  practical correction.
- Do not disclose another person's private information, credentials, private
  material, or uncoordinated security findings.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and licensing or provenance concerns.

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates these expectations, reject
or revert contributions, and restrict participation after repeated or severe
violations. Report conduct issues privately to
[help@tokenhub.com](mailto:help@tokenhub.com). To appeal a moderation decision,
reply through the same channel with the decision and relevant context, and
request review by a maintainer who was not involved in the original decision.
